PAYMENT PLAN

GOAL
Record money received for an invoice, and keep the invoice status correct.

INPUT
- Invoice
- Amount, method (cash, card, UPI, bank transfer), paid date, reference

STEPS
1. Check the invoice exists and is not already fully paid.
2. Check the amount is more than 0.
3. Check the amount is not more than the balance still due.
4. Create the Payment.
5. Balance due = invoice total - sum of all its payments.
6. If balance due is 0, mark the invoice Paid.
   If some money is paid, mark it Partially paid.
7. Do all of this together or not at all (all or nothing).

OVERDUE TRACKING
- Every day, find invoices past their due date with balance due above 0.
- Mark them Overdue.

RULES
- Use Decimal for all money.
- Never delete a payment. To fix a mistake, add a correcting entry.
- Show payments on the invoice page.
