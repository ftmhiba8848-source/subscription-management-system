FINANCE DASHBOARD PLAN

GOAL
Show money numbers on one page. It must load in under 2 seconds,
even with thousands of invoices and payments.

NUMBERS TO SHOW
- Total revenue (all payments received)
- Money still due (unpaid invoice balances)
- Overdue invoices (count and amount)
- Revenue for each month (last 12 months)
- Number of invoices by status (paid, partly paid, overdue)

KEEPING IT FAST
- Do the sums in the database (Sum, Count, group by month), not in Python loops.
- Use select_related for invoice and subscription lookups.
- Add database indexes on invoice status, due date, and payment paid_on.
- Cache the dashboard numbers for 5 minutes.
- Show only the latest 20 invoices in the table, with page numbers.

REPORTS
- Revenue by month
- Overdue invoice list
- Payments by method (cash, card, UPI, bank transfer)
- Option to download as CSV

TESTING
- Create 10,000 test invoices and check the page loads in under 2 seconds.
