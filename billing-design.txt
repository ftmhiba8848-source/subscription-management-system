BILLING MODULE - TABLE DESIGN

TAX
- name
- rate (percent)
- is_active

DISCOUNT
- name
- type (percent or fixed)
- value
- is_active

INVOICE
- subscription (link to Member 2's Subscription)
- period_start
- period_end
- status (Draft / Sent / Paid / Overdue)
- due_date
- subtotal
- tax_total
- discount_total
- total
RULE: subscription + period_start must be unique (never bill twice)

INVOICE LINE
- invoice (link to Invoice)
- description
- quantity
- unit_price
- tax (link to Tax)
- discount (link to Discount)
- line_total

PAYMENT
- invoice (link to Invoice)
- amount
- method
- paid_on
- reference

HOW THEY CONNECT
- 1 Invoice has many Invoice Lines
- 1 Invoice has many Payments
